About Reliance Mall, Salem

Positioned in Meyyanur along Salem's premier Junction Main Road commercial corridor, Reliance Mall stands as the city's definitive one-stop destination, uniting trusted retail anchors, family conveniences, and seamless transit accessibility.

Salem's Premier Shopping Destination

Transforming Family Retail Across Western Tamil Nadu

Established to provide world-class retail conveniences to Salem, Reliance Mall redefines shopping for local families and regional visitors alike. Spanning multiple expansive retail levels, the mall seamlessly combines daily grocery savings at SMART Bazaar, authorized consumer technology at Reliance Digital, trendsetting styles at Reliance Trends, and cozy family cafe dining under a fully climate-controlled atrium.

Strategically located only 1.5 km from the Salem Railway Junction and minutes from the prominent Five Roads intersection, our Meyyanur venue offers covered multi-tier vehicle parking, step-free wheelchair access, and continuous professional security for an enjoyable visit every day.
